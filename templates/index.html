The Problem

Businesses with valid invoices often wait weeks or months for payment, creating working-capital constraints. Traditional invoice financing involves slow underwriting, fragmented verification, limited investor access, high friction, and opaque funding records.

The Solution

AssetFlow allows eligible invoices to become financing assets that investors can fund through a structured marketplace. AI extracts and verifies invoice data, the asset is registered on BOT Chain, and funding transactions are executed transparently on-chain.

How AssetFlow Works

From invoice upload to on-chain funding in five steps.

1

Invoice Upload

Borrowers upload invoice documents (PDF, PNG, JPG) through a secure portal.

↓
2

AI Analysis

Document processing and AI extraction analyze the invoice to extract key fields such as amount, currency, dates, and invoice number.

↓
3

On-Chain Asset

Verified invoices are registered as RWA financing assets on BOT Chain through the AssetRegistry contract.

↓
4

Investor Funding

Investors browse the marketplace and fund assets through the FinancingPool using the configured payment token.

↓
5

Settlement & Records

On-chain AssetFunded events provide immutable funding proof, while local records maintain operational auditability.
