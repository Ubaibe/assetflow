The Problem

Businesses with valid invoices often wait weeks or months for payment, creating working-capital constraints. Traditional invoice financing involves slow underwriting, fragmented verification, limited investor access, high friction, and opaque funding records.

The Solution

AssetFlow allows eligible invoices to become financing assets that investors can fund through a structured marketplace. AI extracts and verifies invoice data, the asset is registered on BOT Chain, and funding transactions are executed transparently on-chain.

How AssetFlow Works

From invoice upload to on-chain funding in five steps.

Smart Contract Architecture

Two core contracts power the AssetFlow financing lifecycle on BOT Chain.

AssetRegistry Asset Identity & Status
↓
FinancingPool Funding & Repayment
↓
USDT Payment Token

AssetRegistry

Registers and tracks financing assets on-chain. Manages asset lifecycle status transitions, originator identity, financing targets, maturity timestamps, and risk scores. The FinancingPool is authorized through this registry.

FinancingPool

Receives investor funding against registered assets, enforces financing caps, and emits AssetFunded events. Also handles originator repayments and pro-rata return claims for investors.

AI-Powered Invoice Analysis

Document processing and extraction reduce manual underwriting effort.

Document Processing

Supports PDF, PNG, and JPG invoice documents with magic-byte validation and MIME type checking.

Field Extraction

Extracts invoice number, face value, currency, issue date, and due date from uploaded documents.

Eligibility Verification

Validates invoice completeness, date ordering, and non-past-due status before on-chain registration.
